# Compliance Certifications & Audit Program

## Current Certifications
- **SOC 2 Type II** — issued annually, covering Security, Availability, and Confidentiality. Available under NDA via the Trust Center.
- **ISO/IEC 27001** — certified, 3-year certification cycle with annual surveillance audits.
- **GDPR** — the Platform acts as a data processor for EU customer data; a DPA is available on request.

## Certifications Not Currently Held
The Platform is **not** FedRAMP authorized, **not** HITRUST certified, and **not** HIPAA compliant out of the box. Customers requiring these should confirm roadmap timing with their account team before relying on the Platform for regulated workloads.

## Audit Cadence
- Internal security audits: quarterly.
- External penetration testing: at least annually, by an independent third party; a summary letter is available under NDA.
- SOC 2 Type II audit: annual, by an independent CPA firm.

## Sharing Audit Artifacts
SOC 2 reports, pen-test summary letters, and the ISO 27001 certificate are shared with prospects and customers under mutual NDA via the Trust Center.
